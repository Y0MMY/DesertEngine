# Nubis Cubed (SIGGRAPH Advances 2023) — Page-by-page catalog, pages 201–208

These are the closing slides of the talk: the Stormbird Encounter wrap-up, the conclusions comparison table, the released asset pack, credits/references, and contacts. No formulas or shader pseudo-code appear anywhere in this range; the technical value is in the reference renders, the method-comparison table, and the asset-pack contents.

## Page 201

- **Header:** "Nubis³ / The Stormbird Encounter". Footer credit: "Atlas / Houdini, 2023".
- **Image:** A Houdini viewport screenshot of the Stormbird Encounter cloud authored as a **voxel/box structure** — the entire cloudscape is shown as thousands of axis-aligned grey cubes at multiple sizes (clearly a sparse voxel / NVDF authoring representation, not a render). The central feature is a large **dome/mound-shaped mass** built from stacked voxel columns — a stepped, roughly hemispherical hill of cubes (the cumulonimbus tower the player flies around in the quest). Around it are terraced voxel walls and a deep dark cavity to the left (the interior corridor/cave the encounter flies through), with flat voxel "floor" layers below and to the right. Shading is plain grey Lambert with a bright specular hotspot lower-right; background regions are black where empty. Viewport chrome ("Persp", "No cam") is visible top-right, confirming a DCC screenshot.
- **What it demonstrates (shape):** the encounter cloud is **freeform 3D-modeled at voxel level in Houdini** — the dimensional profile (dome, walls, tunnel, floors) is explicitly sculpted geometry converted to voxels, not procedurally derived from a 2D coverage/profile signal. This is the "modeling data" stage before noise/erosion detail is applied at render time.
- **Formulas/constants:** none.
- **Speaker notes:** none visible on the slide.

## Page 202

- **Header:** "Nubis³ / The Stormbird Encounter". Footer credit: "Decima Engine, 2023".
- **Image:** The in-engine render of the same encounter cloud, seen from **inside/below the cloud mass** looking up. Cloud shape: a huge soft central **rounded mound** (matching the voxel dome of page 201) surrounded by billowing cauliflower masses on all sides; edges are soft and wispy with fine feathered erosion detail, no visible voxel structure at all — the noise/detail layer fully hides the blocky source.
- **Colors/lighting (ambient reference):** the entire scene is bathed in a **warm beige/taupe ambient** — lit faces are creamy pale-tan/off-white, mid tones are warm grey-brown (roughly mushroom/latte tones), and shadowed undersides go to a desaturated warm grey-brown, not neutral or blue-grey. Two or three small **gaps in the cover reveal saturated sky-blue patches** (top-right a large opening, one small hole centre-upper, faint blue showing lower-left) — the blue reads much cooler and more saturated than any cloud surface, showing that inside the cloud deck the ambient term is dominated by warm multiple-scattered sunlight, while direct sky blue only appears through openings. A bright warm highlight (sun glow through thinner material) sits centre-left near the small hole. Overall contrast is low; nothing is truly dark — even the deepest shadow regions stay mid-value, consistent with heavy multi-scattering/ambient occlusion rather than black shadows.

## Page 204

- **Header:** "Nubis³ / Conclusions".
- **Content:** Comparison table of the three Nubis cloud methods. Columns: **Vertical Profile Method | Envelope Method | Voxel Method**. Rows (green = positive, red = negative, exact cell text quoted):

  | Feature | Vertical Profile Method | Envelope Method | Voxel Method |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Evolution | Yes | Pseudomotion Only | Pseudomotion Only |
  | Time Of Day | Yes | Yes | Yes |
  | Lightning | Yes | No | Yes |
  | Terrain-Cast Shadows | No | Yes | Yes |
  | High Frame-rates | Yes | Yes | Yes |
  | Flight-Capable | No | Yes | Yes |
  | Freeform Modeling | No | No | Yes |
  | Support Quests | No | "Ehhhh..." | Yes |
  | Potential for Growth | No | "Not Really" | "Very Yes" |

- **Key shape-system takeaways:** only the Vertical Profile Method supports true cloud **evolution** (animated formation/dissipation); Envelope and Voxel methods offer **pseudomotion only**. Only the **Voxel Method supports freeform modeling** (sculpting arbitrary cloud shapes, per page 201) and is the direction with growth potential. Vertical Profile is not flight-capable; Envelope and Voxel are.
- **Formulas/constants:** none beyond the table. **Speaker notes:** none.

## Page 205

- **Header:** "Nubis³ / Nubis Voxel Clouds Pack".
- **Content:** Announcement of a released asset pack, link "http://bit.ly/NubisVoxelCloudPack" (with QR code). Two asset groups:
  - **"NVDF's — Parkouring Cloud + Stormbird Cloud, TGA's + VDB"**: two reference renders of the shipped cloud assets against a clear gradient sky (deep saturated blue at top graduating to pale whitish-blue at the horizon).
    - Left (Parkouring Cloud): a low, elongated cumulus with two lobed towers, brilliant white sunlit tops, neutral-to-slightly-blue soft grey shadowed underside, flat-ish darker base, crisp cauliflower lobes with soft wispy skirts at the ends.
    - Right (Stormbird Cloud): a larger, wider cumulus congestus mass; bright white crisp upper lobes, broad **blue-grey shadowed core/underside** (the shadow tint clearly picks up sky blue in these full-sky renders — contrast with the warm interior ambient of page 202), ragged eroded fringes at the base.
  - **"Voxel Cloud Noise — TGA's + VDB + Generator"**: four greyscale tiling noise textures shown as thumbnails:
    1. Wispy/curly filament noise (curl-like strands, high frequency, black voids) — reads as the "wispy" detail type.
    2. Higher-contrast clumpy/billow noise with large black holes — a billow/erosion type.
    3. Inverted-Worley-style cellular web (bright cells with dark vein boundaries).
    4. Denser, finer cellular/bubbly noise (bright puffs packed together, dark cracks).
    Together these are the detail-noise set used to erode/detail the voxel cloud density (the classic Nubis wispy-vs-billowy detail duality, distributed as both TGA slices and VDB plus a generator).
- **Formulas/constants:** none (URL only). **Speaker notes:** none.

## Page 206

- **Header:** "Nubis³ / Conclusions". Footer: "Decima Engine, 2023".
- **Image:** Hero in-game shot from Horizon Forbidden West/Burning Shores: Aloy riding a Sunwing (green-winged flying machine) in the foreground, above a tropical coastal landscape with a **volcano (glowing orange lava)** lower-left. Behind: a towering wall of cumulus/cumulonimbus clouds filling the frame top-to-bottom.
- **Cloud/color description:** warm late-afternoon lighting. Cloud tops are bright warm white/cream with strong cauliflower lobe definition; shaded lobe undersides are warm grey with a subtle mauve/dusty tint; the lower cloud bank near the horizon becomes hazier and picks up a warm golden atmospheric-perspective tint. Deep crevices between lobes hold soft mid-grey shadow — again no crushed blacks, high ambient fill. The sky visible at top edge is pale warm blue. Edge quality: crisp rounded lobes with soft micro-detail, distant banks progressively softened by haze.
