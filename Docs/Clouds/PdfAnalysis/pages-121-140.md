## Page 121
**Slide: Light-energy decomposition diagram.** Black background schematic: a yellow sun at upper left with a yellow arrow toward a gray cartoon cumulus; three light-blue arrows pointing DOWN/inward onto the cloud from the sky (from above-left, above-right, and from the right toward a second smaller cloud); a red lightning bolt under the cloud base with small red arrows radiating UP into the cloud.

On-slide text (color-coded):
`Light Energy = Direct Scattering + Ambient Scattering + Secondary Scattering`
- "Direct Scattering" in yellow (sun),
- "Ambient Scattering" in light blue/lavender (sky arrows),
- "Secondary Scattering" in red (energy re-entering from below, e.g. bounce/emissive sources).

No formulas beyond the sum; no constants. This is the framing slide for the whole lighting section: their cloud light model is the sum of these three terms.

## Page 136
**Slide: The multiple-scattering fix + code.** Same cloud/camera as page 135, now rendered bright: white-pink luminous core, soft warm-white lobes, only mild shading — the fixed result. Bottom black band contains the code (quoted exactly):

```
    ms_volume = dimensional_profile;
cloud_distance = GetVoxelCloudDistance(inSamplePosition);
    ms_volume *= exp(-inSunLightSummedDensitySamples * Remap(sun_dot, 0.0, 0.9, 0.25, ValueRemap(cloud_distance, -128.0, 0.0, 0.05, 0.25)));
```

Interpretation of visible identifiers/constants:
- `ms_volume` (multiple-scattering volume term) is **seeded from `dimensional_profile`** — the cloud's shape/profile field feeds the MS estimate.
- `cloud_distance = GetVoxelCloudDistance(inSamplePosition)` — signed distance to the cloud surface from the voxel structure (negative inside; range used: -128..0).
- Attenuation: `exp(-inSunLightSummedDensitySamples * k)` where `inSunLightSummedDensitySamples` is the summed density toward the sun (from the 256x256x32 voxel cache) and the exponent scale `k = Remap(sun_dot, 0.0, 0.9, 0.25, ValueRemap(cloud_distance, -128.0, 0.0, 0.05, 0.25))`.
  - `sun_dot` in [0.0, 0.9] remaps from 0.25 down/up to a distance-dependent value;
  - the inner `ValueRemap(cloud_distance, -128.0, 0.0, 0.05, 0.25)` makes the coefficient 0.05 deep inside the cloud (distance -128) and 0.25 at the surface (distance 0) — i.e. deep samples attenuate LESS (more multiple scattering / light retention deep inside), surface samples attenuate more, and the effect is angle-dependent via `sun_dot`.
