## Page 65

**Header:** `Nubis³ / Voxel Clouds` — ray-march diagram slide (black background).

**Diagram:** Classic Nubis ray-march schematic: camera at lower left, a grey cloud blob at right, a yellow sun at upper left. The primary ray from the camera passes through the cloud with red filled dots at sample points inside the cloud (empty red circles before the cloud = samples where density is zero/cheap), and a red **X** past the cloud where the march terminates (full opacity reached). From each in-cloud sample, secondary rays (with their own red sample dots) march toward the sun.

**Text (the per-sample work list, annotated at the first in-cloud sample):**
- `Sample Secondary Light Energy`
- `Sample Ambient Light Energy`
- `Sample Direct Light Energy ( + second ray-march )` — "+ second ray-march" is highlighted in red, i.e. the sun-shadow march per sample
- `Sample Density`

Key takeaway: at every ray-march step the renderer samples **density**, **direct light** (which itself costs a secondary ray-march toward the sun), **ambient light energy**, and **secondary (multi-scatter) light energy**. Ambient is an explicit, separately-sampled energy term, on equal footing with direct light — not an afterthought constant.

**Formulas/constants:** none beyond the list above.

**Speaker notes:** none present in extraction.

## Page 78

**Header:** `Nubis³ / Voxel Clouds / Modeling` — black table slide.

**Table (exact contents; red = rejected as too large, green + white box = chosen):**

| Resolution (voxels) | Uncompressed (2 bytes / texel) | BC4 (0.5 byte / texel) |
|---|---|---|
| 4096 x 4096 x 512 | 17.178 Gigabytes (red) | 4.294 Gigabytes (red) |
| 2048 x 2048 x 256 | 2.146 Gigabytes (red) | 536.870 Megabytes (red) |
| 1024 x 1024 x 128 | 268.434 Megabytes (red) | 67.108 Megabytes (red) |
| 512 x 512 x 64 | 33.554 Megabytes (red) | **8.388 Megabytes** (green, boxed = selected) |

Key facts:
- Uncompressed voxel density = **2 bytes/texel** (16-bit); compressed with **BC4** = **0.5 byte/texel** (4:1).
- The shipped density-field storage is **512 × 512 × 64 voxels, BC4, ≈ 8.388 MB**.
- Against the 4 km × 4 km × 500 m volume of page 76, 512×512×64 means one voxel ≈ **7.8 m × 7.8 m × 7.8 m** — far too coarse on its own for close-up detail, which is exactly why the Density Sampler later gains additional modeling/detail steps (noise-based detail added at sample time on subsequent pages of the deck).
